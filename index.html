FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is OAU eCampus?
OAU eCampus is the online campus of Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife. It delivers OAU's accredited programmes fully online to learners across Nigeria, West Africa and beyond, in partnership with Astria Learning.
Is an OAU eCampus degree the same as an on-campus degree?
Yes. You study OAU programmes under OAU's academic authority, and your degree is awarded by Obafemi Awolowo University. certificate wording
Who awards my degree, OAU or Astria?
OAU. Astria Learning provides the online platform and learner support; OAU sets the curriculum, teaches, examines and awards the degree.
Who can study with OAU eCampus?
Working professionals, OAU alumni, diaspora learners and anyone who meets the entry requirements for their programme. entry requirements
Can I study while working full-time?
Yes. Programmes are designed for working adults: study in the evenings and at weekends, on your phone or laptop. weekly study hours
What if my internet or power is unreliable?
You can download resources from the 170,000+ item Astria Digital Library and study offline, then sync when you're back online.
Can I apply from outside Nigeria?
Yes. OAU eCampus is open to learners across West Africa and the diaspora, with 24/7 support across time zones. countries and fee currency
How do I apply?
Download the prospectus for your programme, then complete the online application. An advisor can guide you through each step. application portal link

Still have questions?

Speak to an advisor from our Student Success team. We'll help you choose a programme and walk you through applying.
